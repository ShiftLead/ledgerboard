# TD-058: v2.7, the Status release, and `migrate`

v2.7 is the release that finishes moving every ledger to the v2 form. It ships `migrate`, a tool that rewrites a
ledger's old forms into the new ones in one commit, and then makes the old Status words an error.

What `migrate` does to each entry:
- An archived entry's Status becomes one of `Resolved`, `Superseded` or `Dropped` with its date (`Done` and
  `Built` become `Resolved`, `OBE` becomes `Dropped`, and so on). A word it cannot map, or a date it cannot find,
  stops it: it lists them and writes nothing, and the session that knows the repo decides each.
- An open entry's stage word (`Designed`, `Built`, …) or prose Status becomes `Open`, with the old text kept as a
  dated Log line. It sets `Kind` only if the caller says which stage means which kind, since those words belong to
  the consumers, not to ledgerboard.
- Notes after a Status's word and comments after an `Added` date move into the Log, in date order.
- `Location` moves from the header into the body.

After it, a Status outside the four words is an error on every entry, so a repo vendors v2.7 and runs `migrate`
in the same PR. `migrate` stays in every v2.x and goes in v3.0, once every repo's dry run shows nothing left.

The choices for Paul: whether the closed set is an error everywhere (recommended) or only on changed entries;
whether `migrate` sets `Kind` from a map the caller passes (recommended) or from words ledgerboard carries; and
whether `Location` moves to the body at all. Where it stands: a scope draft, not yet reviewed, waiting on Paul's
answer on the board.
