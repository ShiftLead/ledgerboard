# TD-075: any offered `<head>:` answer is a form the person's words fill

A board item's answers are complete answers, except a look's `Not right: <what>`, which the person fills with
their own words. Nothing else works that way. A design's end-of-rounds `Hold: <note>` gets pressed as written, so
the board records the placeholder. When a consumer does fill it (`Hold: wait for the vendor`), no reader knows it
is the `Hold` answer. So an item whose `Lifts:` lists the form is never lifted or closed by a fill. Even the look's
form is known only to two helpers nothing calls. ShiftLead's design TD-1557 needs the general form, and its build
TD-1698 waits on this repo's format.

**What a form is.** An offered answer whose label is a head, a colon, and nothing or one `<…>` placeholder:
`Hold: <note>`, `Hold:`, `Other :  <what>`, `Not right: <what>`. Words after the colon (`Not right: wrong repo`) or
two placeholders make a complete answer, as today. The head is the text before the colon, trimmed, and heads
compare in any case.

**How it is filled.** An answer fills a form when it is none of the item's complete answers and its text before
its first colon is that form's head. The fill is written as the head as offered, a colon, one space, and the
words, so `hold:wait` becomes `Hold: wait`. Empty words, or only a placeholder, are refused: *Hold needs its
words*. Where two forms share a head, the first fills.

**The functions.** Three new pure functions: `is_form()`, `form_head()` and `fill_form(said, offered, form=None)`.
With `form`, the caller names the form: the head is put back where the words lack it (`the vendor` becomes
`Hold: the vendor`). `is_not_right_form()` and `fill_not_right()` stay, as the look's case of the general form. So
`not right: <what>` and `Not right : <what>` now count as the look's form, and `Not right:grey` comes back as
`Not right: grey`.

**What lifts.** An answer lifts when it is one of `Lifts:` as today, or when the form it fills is listed there. A
complete answer that only looks like a fill (`Hold: later`, offered whole beside `Hold: <note>`) lifts only by
equality. Every reader asks the same function, so closing on the answer, the decide lift, the gate sweep and the
merge gate all agree.

**What the writers do.** The writers fill the answer once, before anything is written, so the board's `Decided:`,
the ledger's `gate lifted` line and the commit subject all carry the same text, and the merge gate's check that
they match still passes. They refuse a form sent back unfilled. On a `decide`, two forms with one head, or a form
marked ` (default)`, is refused by the writer and flagged by the board check. The flag applies only to an open
item not yet decided, but `fmt` also names it on lines nobody edited, which is deliberate. `decided_line()` drops a
default mark from a form and writes the person's words as given.

**The release.** A minor, in the last v2.x minor alongside TD-078's first two builds, since TD-070 allows no v2.x
release after its first build. One thing reads differently on lines already written: a `Decided:` that fills a
form listed in its `Lifts:` now lifts, where it read as a work order; and `not right: …` in lower case, or
`Not right : …` with a space before the colon, becomes the look's form where it was a complete answer. This repo's board has no `Lifts:` field; each
consumer searches its own board before vendoring. The release notes also list the writers' new refusals. A
consumer writes fills only once every reader of its board runs the minor, so agent-cadence's vendor PR comes before
ShiftLead's TD-1698. On the `--board-only` route the caller must pass the same filled text to the ledger and the
board. If it misses the last v2.x minor, it ships in v3.0 and TD-1698 waits for v3.0. One build, B1, with its SPEC text,
code and fixtures. When this repo's other builds are filed, TD-070's first build also waits on this one, its
`_lifting()` callers pass the item's answers, and TD-078's two builds take a three-build version-number rule, so
all three name one minor.

**Alternatives weighed.** A marker of its own for forms (`Hold: …`) would be explicit, but it would rewrite every
look line and every design item already written. Making a form only where `Lifts:` lists it would leave a
work-order form such as `Other: <what>` unfillable. Folding the two look helpers into `fill_form()` would be
tidier, but they are public.

**Left out.** More answers on a look. Waiting-only forms: `Not yet: <why>` is an ordinary form, and its fill
neither keeps waiting nor lifts unless `Lifts:` lists it. Reading a form's words for anything beyond the fill.
Removing `NOT_RIGHT_FORM_RE`.
