- **Context:** In ledgerboard (the ledger and board formats agent-cadence and ShiftLead both use), TD-119 lets the gate sweep offer an entry's own answers. Today, when an entry waits on you and nothing asks about it, the sweep files a question that always offers Go or Not yet, even where the entry asks something else (two ShiftLead entries on this board today, one asking one more round or go, one asking Works or Not right). The design: an entry may list its answers in an Answers section and which of them lift its hold in a Lifts section; the sweep offers them when they are usable and Go or Not yet otherwise; the ledger check flags unusable answers on an entry that waits on you. Any answer other than a lifting one or Not yet becomes an instruction to the entry's session, which closes the question when done and asks again itself; the sweep then waits out its re-ask window, 30 days by default. Two rounds: problems that would change the build, per design review, 2 then 0. The closing check found one more, now fixed: a Lifts line written without a blank line before it would have been silently read as part of an answer, so the reader now refuses it. That fix has been read by no design reviewer. Plain-words summary: docs/synopses/TD-119.md; the review record: docs/reviews/TD-119.md (PR #357).
- **Question:** Should the TD-119 design get a third review round on the closing check's fix, or be built as it stands?
- **Recommended:** One more round — the closing check's fix adds a rule no design reviewer has read, and another round costs little.
