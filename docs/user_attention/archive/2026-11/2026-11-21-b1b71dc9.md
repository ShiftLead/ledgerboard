The design keeps the sweep asking, but its question now names the older items and says only an answer on it counts; the merge check's two refusals name the item that does ask; and it states that closing a to-do step never releases an entry. It ran four review rounds; problems that would change the build, per round: 7, 6, 2, 0, and the closing check 0. Round 4's six small fixes and the closing check's one have been read by no design reviewer. Plain-words summary: docs/synopses/TD-152.md; the review record: docs/reviews/TD-152.md.
- **Question:** With round 4 finding nothing that changes the build and its small fixes unread by a reviewer, should the design get one more review round before it is built?

Otherwise Go, if you accept those fixes as the reviews' own suggestions; Hold with a note, if you want the design changed.
