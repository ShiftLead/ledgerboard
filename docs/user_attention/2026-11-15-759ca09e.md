## Should the rule that lets the gate sweep offer an entry's own answers get a fourth review round before it is built?

**Kind:** decide
**Added:** 2026-11-15
**Session:** lb-designer
**Context:** TD-119 decision (paul)
**Due:** 2026-11-22
**Answers:** One more round (default) — a session runs one more review round, then asks again | Go — the grinders build it | Hold: <note> — a session revises the design as your note says

- **Context:** In ledgerboard (the ledger and board formats agent-cadence and ShiftLead both use), TD-119 lets the gate sweep offer an entry's own answers. Today, when an entry waits on you and nothing asks about it, the sweep files a question that always offers Go or Not yet, even where the entry asks something else. The design: an entry may list its answers in an Answers section and which of them lift its hold in a Lifts section; the sweep offers them when they are usable, and Go or Not yet otherwise; the ledger check flags unusable answers on an entry that waits on you. You bought a third round. Its checks and design review found seven problems that would change the build, all fixed: example answers the writers would refuse, an empty Lifts section read two ways, test cases filed where they could not show what they claim, a release note the two v3.3 builds would have shared and contradicted, and, three times, text stuck to the answer sections being misread. That last is a topic that keeps coming back: each fix closed one way and left another. The reviewer judged the design sound and the boundary rule the weak point; round 3 replaced the patches with two rules over the whole entry (an Answers or Lifts line stuck to the line above is refused; each section is one paragraph). If another round still finds a hole there, the fallback is to make each section a single line. Problems that would change the build, per design review, rounds 1 to 3: 2, 0, 3. None of round 3's fixes has been read by a design reviewer. Plain-words summary: docs/synopses/TD-119.md; the review record: docs/reviews/TD-119.md (PR #370).
- **Question:** Should the TD-119 design get a fourth review round on round 3's fixes, or be built as it stands?
- **Recommended:** One more round — the last design review found three problems that would change the build, and the closing check one more, all in the part that keeps coming back; none of the fixes has been read by a design reviewer. **Otherwise:** Go, if you accept the fixes as checked against the code only; Hold with a note, if you want the design changed, for example each section made a single line.
