**Round 1.** One review (R1), design and fact-check together, running the readers and writers on invented entries.
It confirmed every symptom in *The problem*, the split's carry and `note`'s Log past the rule, and that every reader
named reaches the body through `_entry_parts()`. Three build-changing (F1–F3: how the rule travels through the
writers, its gap, and comments), seven minor; all applied.

**Round 2.** One review (R2), design and fact-check together, prototyping `_closing_rule()` and the writers. It
confirmed the plumbing and the callers; two build-changing (F11, F12: a rule-only body after a write, and readers
that drop comments first), both the rest of round 1's topics, refined and not undone; six minor. All applied.

**Round 3.** One review (R3), prototyping both reading paths and fuzzing them. Three build-changing (F19–F21): a
comment crossing the cut, the two paths still disagreeing (reopening F12), and the blank-line insertion. **A
recurring topic: comments around the rule** (F3, F12, F19, F20). Positions: round 1 read the rule on raw text,
round 2 with comments aside line by line; both left shapes where a comment spans the cut. Round 3's fix decides the
rule once, on the comment-dropped text both paths can make, which makes the paths agree by construction rather
than by enumerating shapes. Two minor, applied.
