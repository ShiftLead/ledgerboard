## TD-147: Two delegated ledger-writer fixtures stop at the wrong refusal

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** Debt
**Added:** 2026-11-18
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 27bb175a948e4d4f878235dc841991d0

**Why:** The techlead's read of #436 (TD-146) noted that the ledger writer's fixtures `set_delegated_override_refused` and `set_delegated_reassign_refused` pass no `--answer`, so both stop at the earlier refusal *--delegate needs an explicit --answer* and never reach the override lift's or the reassign's refusal they are named for. Both are still refused, so nothing is wrong in the code; but the cases do not test what their names say, and a change to the override or reassign route would go unnoticed by them.

**Location:** fixtures/ledger_writer/

**Fix:** Give each case `--answer Go` beside `--delegate` and `--scope`, so the delegate passes the explicit-answer rule and the write reaches the override lift's refusal (*an override lift takes --by and its why, no --answer, board flag or --delegate*) and the reassign's (*a reassign takes --by and --why, no --answer, board flag, --override or --delegate*); regenerate their `expected.json`.

**Done when:** Each of the two cases' `expected.json` records the refusal its name says, and the suite, the fixtures and ruff pass.
