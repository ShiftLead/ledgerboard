## TD-034: `board.py decide` writes the board alone, so answering a gate's item still leaves the entry blocked

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** 2e9dce7d0dea
**Blocked by:** TD-033
**Part of:** TD-010
**Location:** ledgerboard/board.py (decide); SPEC.md The board writer; fixtures (writer)

**Why:** v2.3's `decide --by` records the answer on the board only (§1.1, *Releases*). The decide lift writes the board and the ledger in one write, and the `set by=` route needs the board half alone (target design §1.1, §6).

**Fix:** Per target design §1.1 and §6:
- `board.py decide --by <person>` as the two-file lift: the board line through `edit_line(…, close=closes(…))` and the ledger entries through `decide_lift`, written both or neither, with the board's `People:` and `Answered by:` checks;
- a waiting-only answer closes the item and logs `kept waiting`;
- a cross-repo Context writes the board alone and leaves the item decided-but-open;
- `board.py decide --board-only` writes the board half alone (the `set by=` route);
- `decided_line(…, by=)`.

**Done when:** The two-file `decide` and `--board-only` are built with SPEC text and writer fixtures (a lift, a kept-waiting answer, a parent snapshot, a cross-repo item, an either/or item lifted on its non-default label), and the suite passes.
