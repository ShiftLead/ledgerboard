## TD-119: The gate sweep offers Go or Not yet even where the waiting entry asks for other answers

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-15
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** efbdbf03166e4053a30413b75d4a733e
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)

**Why:** The gate sweep's item for an uncovered decision always offers `Go | Not yet` (`SWEEP_DECISION_ANSWERS`, set in `sweep()`), whatever the entry's Ask asks. In PR #355 (2026-11-15, shiftlead/shiftlead's sweep onto this board) two items asked something else. TD-1879's Ask was *one more round, or go ahead now?* and named Hold too. TD-1404's was *Works* or *Not right: <what it printed>*. Both offered Go | Not yet, so the person cannot press the answer the question asks for. Since the lifts default to Go, a *Works* typed as a reply would leave the gate standing. ShiftLead's gatesweep.py vendors this module and files `sweep()`'s answers unchanged, so the fix is this package's (ShiftLead's techlead, m-988ff211d5bb).

**Ask:** Should the one-line rule that lets the gate sweep offer an entry's own answers be built now, its sixth and last review round having found nothing that would change the build?

Go: a session files its one build and tells ShiftLead it can write answer lines. One more round: a session runs a seventh review round, past the cap, on round 6's small fixes, then asks again. Hold with a note: nothing is built, and a session revises the design as the note says.

**Fix:** Designed: [docs/plans/TD-119-entry-answers.md](../plans/TD-119-entry-answers.md) (rounds: [docs/reviews/TD-119.md](../reviews/TD-119.md); in plain words: [docs/synopses/TD-119.md](../synopses/TD-119.md)). An entry's own answers are one-line body fields, `**Answers:**` and `**Lifts:**`, in the board's `Answers:` value form; with no `**Lifts:**`, `Go` lifts; a field that runs past its line, a lead that starts no section, and a field inside the other's line are refused (D1, Draft 4 after Paul's hold; Draft 5 keeps all three this reader's beside TD-124's flag, and finds a field inside the line by an overlapping scan; Draft 6 also refuses two answers with one label, case folded). One reader, `entry_answers()`, decides whether they can be offered, its last check `item_file()` itself (D2). The gate sweep offers them for an uncovered decision, else `Go | Not yet` (D3); `check()`'s `answers-unusable` flags a gated entry whose answers cannot be offered, and the writer refuses an edit that makes it (D4); the answers are held to rule 2 at a consumer's writer (D5). A minor release, v3.3, untagged, shared with TD-123 and TD-133 (D6). The section-edge rule is TD-124's; whichever build merges second moves `entry_answers()` onto its line reader. One build, filed on Paul's go, `Part of` this entry; then a note to ShiftLead's question seat (D6 *The consumers*).

**Done when:** A design names where an entry's answers live, how the sweep's item carries them and their lifts, and what it does without them, filed as builds; or it records why the generic answers stay.
