## TD-018: A board names no person, so nothing can check who may answer it, and an answer records no one

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-02
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** 2e9dce7d0dea
**Blocked by:** TD-017
**Part of:** TD-008
**Location:** ledgerboard (see Fix); SPEC.md; fixtures

**Why:** No board declares whose it is, and `Decided:` does not say who answered, so the lift rules of v2.4 have nothing to check against, and a team board cannot exist (target design §6.2).

**Fix:** Target design §6.2: the `People:` preamble line, `board_people(content)`, `board_problems`' preamble and Context-person checks, `Decided: <label> (by <person>) (D).` with `BoardItem.decided_by` and the named reading change, `decide --by` (defaulting to a one-person board's name, refusing a name off `People:`, and proceeding grammar-only on a board with none), and the read-backs; SPEC text and fixtures.

**Done when:** `People:` and `(by …)` are read and written as §6.2 says, with fixtures for each rule, and the suite passes.
