## TD-115: A merge-gate verdict for a PR that only lifts a gate, with no board answer

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-15 (agent-cadence's grinder, through ao td add; drafted by ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 7d2eb7aca8034b73989fd432f2fe7d3a
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)

**Why:** A lift made with ShiftLead's Lift the hold press cannot pass the merge gate today. The press (ShiftLead TD-1829, its design 4.4-host-agent.md, merged in ShiftLead/shiftlead#3820, with TD-1831 as agent-cadence's grinder reports) lands one commit and one PR titled `shiftlead: lift decision (<p>) on TD-n (session n/a)`, whose diff is `plan_lift(led, 'TD-n', 'decision (<p>)', <p>, answer=...)` alone, and by ShiftLead's design it writes no board line. `lift_pr_verdict()` passes only a PR that answers one board item (a `Decided:`, a `decide` subject, one commit) and whose ledger change is exactly `decide_lift()`'s, so a ledger-only lift goes to review at `lift-board-diff`; agent-cadence's own board-append carve-out (in its `check_cadence.py`, not in this package) takes only a PR that changes the board alone. So such a PR fits neither, and agent-cadence's audit fails its review row and lists the PR as merged outside step 5 (agent-cadence TD-262).

The verdicts belong here: `lift_pr_verdict()`, `tripwire()` and the subject grammar (`EDIT_SUBJECT_RE`, `edit_action()`) are this format's, pure functions over the trees a consumer passes in, so a lift-only verdict fits beside them without the package learning git or the forge. Asked for by agent-cadence's grinder (ao-agent-cadence-ac-grinder, m-64408e83bd55, 2026-11-15); agent-cadence wires it into its `check_cadence.py` as carve-out `lift` once a release carrying it is vendored.

What is open, and why the design comes first: a lift with no covering board item would pass on its Log line alone. That line is the press's only trace, and it is forgeable (SPEC *The merge gate's verdicts* says the tripwire is forgeable and review is its backstop). A carve-out skips ordinary review, so the design must settle what stands in for the person's own answer on the board before any PR is let through on this shape alone.

**Ask:** Should the merge rule that lets the Lift the hold button's change merge on its own get an eighth review round, or be built now?
