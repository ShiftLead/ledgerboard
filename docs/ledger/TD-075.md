**Why:** A board item's offered answers are either complete answers or one form: the live look's `Not right: <what>` (`Not right:` alone or with one `<…>`, matched case-sensitively), which the person fills with their words. Even that form is known only to `is_not_right_form` and `fill_not_right`, which no reader in the module calls (only `fixtures/run.py` and the tests). So a design's end-of-rounds `Hold: <note>` is pressed by a consumer as written and lands on the board as its placeholder, and when a consumer does fill it, `_lifting` — and through it `closes()`, `decide_lift`, `edit_line`'s close, the gate sweep and `uncovered_decisions` — compares the filled words literally against `Lifts:`, so they never match a form listed there; a filled `Not right: …` fares the same. ShiftLead's design TD-1557 (PR ShiftLead/shiftlead#3511, its rounds ended, not yet merged) generalises the look's case: any offered `<head>:` or `<head>: <…>` is a form the person's words fill. The forms are this repo's board format: ShiftLead and agent-cadence read the board only through vendored ledgerboard, so until `SPEC.md`, the module and its fixtures know the general form, ShiftLead's build TD-1698 cannot start; it waits on the release that carries it, vendored by agent-cadence's pin bump.

Asked for by ShiftLead's designer session (ao-shiftlead-designer-ao-1, mail m-d367e8661108, from TD-1557's Handed upstream section). What it asked for: `is_form(label)`, `form_head(label)` and `fill_form(said, offered, form=None)`, over labels taken after the consequence split with the ` (default)` mark off (the head matched in any case, spaces before the colon ignored; with `form`, that form's head put back where the words lack it, then that form filled and no other; an empty fill refused as *<head> needs its words*; the fill written as the head as offered, one space, the words on one line; the first matching form wins on a standing line; a non-string `said`, or a `form` out of range or naming a plain answer, refused); `_lifting` reading a filled form as its form, so `decide_lift`, `closes()`, `edit_line`'s close and the gate sweep agree; `item_problem()` (so `board_problems()`) and `item_line()` refusing two forms whose heads match in any case and a form marked `(default)`, on an open `decide` only, while `decided_line()` copies a ledger entry's form with its ` (default)` mark stripped; `is_not_right_form` and `fill_not_right` becoming the look's case of the general form (any-case head, so `"not right: <what>"` is a form; its refusal reading *Not right needs its words*); new fixtures; SPEC *The board*'s `Answers:` bullet and the grammar appendix. It leaves the version call to this repo: `closes()` would read a filled form as its form on lines that already exist, a reading change.
