## TD-075: Generalise the look's Not right form: any offered <head>: or <head>: <…> answer is a form the person's words fill

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-11 (ao-shiftlead-designer-ao-1, through ao td add --repo; drafted by ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** a2c1907b118446c89e78063e60e5b5a6
**Location:** SPEC.md (The board: the Answers: bullet; Versions; Appendix: grammar, look-answers and the answer rule); ledgerboard/__init__.py (NOT_RIGHT, NOT_RIGHT_FORM_RE, is_not_right_form, fill_not_right, _lifting, decide_lift, closes, edit_line, item_problem, item_line, decided_line); ledgerboard/board.py (add, decided=); fixtures/edit/ (not_right_fills, not_right_forms), the fixtures manifest

**Why:** A board item's offered answers are either complete answers, pressed as they are, or one form: the live look's `Not right: <what>`, which the person fills with their words (`is_not_right_form`, `fill_not_right`). Every other answer whose label is a head and a placeholder is pressed as written, so a design's end-of-rounds `Hold: <note>` lands on the board as its placeholder rather than the person's note, and its `closes()` and `decide_lift` read it as a label nothing offered. ShiftLead's design TD-1557 (PR ShiftLead/shiftlead#3511, still in its rounds) generalises the look's case: any offered `<head>:` or `<head>: <…>` is a form the person's words fill. The forms are this repo's board format: ShiftLead and agent-cadence read the board only through vendored ledgerboard, so until `SPEC.md`, the module and its fixtures know the general form, ShiftLead's build TD-1698 cannot start; it waits on the release that carries it, vendored by agent-cadence's pin bump.

Asked for by ShiftLead's designer session (ao-shiftlead-designer-ao-1, mail m-d367e8661108, from TD-1557's Handed upstream section). What it asked for: `is_form(label)`, `form_head(label)` and `fill_form(said, offered, form=None)` (the head matched in any case, spaces before the colon ignored; an empty fill refused as *<head> needs its words*; the fill written as the head as offered, one space, the words on one line; the first matching form wins on a standing line; a bad `form` index or a non-string `said` refused); `_lifting` reading a filled form as its form, so `decide_lift`, `closes()`, `edit_line`'s close, the gate sweep and the tripwires agree; `item_problem()` and `item_line()` refusing two forms whose heads match in any case and a form marked `(default)`, on an open `decide` only, while `decided_line()` copies a ledger entry's form with its ` (default)` mark stripped; `is_not_right_form` and `fill_not_right` becoming the look's case of the general form (any-case head; `"not right: <what>"` a form); new fixtures; SPEC *The board*'s `Answers:` bullet and the grammar appendix. It leaves the version call to this repo: `closes()` would read a filled form as its form on lines that already exist, a reading change.

**Fix:** A design round first (Kind design-first, cadence.md §4.8): a plan in `docs/plans/` that settles, against ShiftLead's request above and SPEC *The board* and *Versions*, (1) the form's grammar — what makes a label a form after the consequence split, how a head is compared, how a fill is written; (2) the API (`is_form`, `form_head`, `fill_form`, and `is_not_right_form`/`fill_not_right` as the look's case, kept or folded); (3) the refusals in `item_problem()`, `item_line()` and `decided_line()`; (4) how `_lifting`, and through it `decide_lift`, `closes()`, `edit_line`'s close and the gate sweep, read a filled form; (5) the version call — whether reading a filled form as its form on lines already written is a minor (v2.8 or later) or a major, and what the release notes say; (6) the fixtures, including the changed `not_right_fills` and `not_right_forms` cases. Then Paul's go on the board, and its builds filed `Part of` this entry. After the release (Paul's): agent-cadence's vendor PR, then ShiftLead's TD-1698.
