## TD-021: Release v2.3, which the consumers' vendor PRs and v2.4's builds wait on

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Owner:** paul
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** 2e9dce7d0dea
**Location:** release.sh; the release header

**Why:** The consumers' v2.3 work waits on a release, not on the last build. TD-008 archives with its last build (TD-019), before anyone stamps and tags v2.3, so a consumer's vendor entry blocked by TD-019 or TD-008 would become pickable with no release to vendor. This entry is the release itself, so each consumer's vendor entry (agent-cadence#TD-213) and the filing of v2.4's builds (TD-010, TD-011) can wait on it.

**Fix:** Once TD-012 to TD-019 are merged: `./release.sh 2.3` on a branch, its PR through the usual review and merge, then `./release.sh --tag 2.3` from a checkout at origin/main. The release notes name every v2.3 change by its entry (target design §7.1's v2.3 row) and the one-time item-key exception (§6). Archiving this entry is the signal the vendors and v2.4 wait for.

**Done when:** The `v2.3` tag is on origin, its header passes `python3 fixtures/run.py --check-header`, and this entry is archived.
