## TD-136: Carry an entry's context onto the item the gate sweep files for it

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** grinder
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** a87e09396ed04f0c913def8d654108e6
**Part of:** TD-124

**Why:** TD-124 moves what an Ask of several paragraphs holds after its question into a `**Context:**` section. The gate sweep's item carries the Ask's line as its text but nothing from that Context, so an item about a migrated entry loses the context the person needs to answer it.

**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py (sweep, _sweep_item); SPEC.md; fixtures/

**Fix:** Build [docs/plans/TD-124-body-grammar.md](../plans/TD-124-body-grammar.md) *Builds* (B4) and *Not done*: `sweep()` gives the item it files for an entry's gate the entry's `**Context:**` section as its detail block's context, read by today's section reader (TD-133 adds the `--context` writer); an entry with no Context section gives the item no context, as today. Beyond the plan's B4 text, added by this entry: the `SPEC.md` passage on the sweep's item gains one sentence saying so, with its *Versions* line, and a fixture under the sweep's cases shows an entry with an Ask and a Context and the item it makes. Building it before TD-134 keeps this repo's three multi-paragraph Asks from losing their context on an item.

**Done when:** One PR has merged with `sweep()` carrying an entry's `**Context:**` as its item's detail context, the `SPEC.md` sentence with the code, the new sweep fixture passing, and the suite, fixtures and ruff green.
