## TD-024: item_problem() passes a line with a tab or other non-space whitespace after its `- [ ] `

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** ao-ledgerboard-lb-grinder
**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py item_problem(); SPEC.md Appendix: grammar; fixtures/edit

**Why:** SPEC's *The form the writers write* says an item line opens `- [ ] ` at column 0, single-spaced, and `item_problem()` checks only `line.startswith("- [ ] ")` and not `"- [ ]  "`. So a line such as `- [ ] <tab>act 2026-01-05 (n/a) — x. Context: TD-7. Due: 2026-01-20.` (a tab, `\r` or U+2028 after the box) passes the gate, though no writer writes it: `ITEM_RE`'s `\s+` and the rebuilt `want` both drop that whitespace. Found by TD-019's grammar appendix review (PR #77), whose EBNF refuses it and names the gap in prose.

**Fix:** `item_problem()` refuses an item line whose `- [ ] ` is followed by any further whitespace, with the `open:` rule's message; a fixture in `fixtures/edit/` (item_problems) for a tab, and the prose sentence under SPEC's *The open item line* grammar block dropped. The gate judges only lines it has not seen (`board_problems(board, old)`), so no board on a consumer gains a fault.

**Done when:** The gate refuses it, with a fixture, and the appendix's prose note is gone; the suite and the fixtures pass.
