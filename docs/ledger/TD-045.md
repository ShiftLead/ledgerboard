**Location:** SPEC.md *Events* (the check table), ledgerboard/__init__.py (`parse_check()`, `CHECK_TYPES`), fixtures/, docs/plans/target-design.md §4.2 and §7

**Why:** An event gate takes one of v2.4's three typed checks (`pr-merged`, `file-contains`, `ref-includes`). The target design (§4, its *Events are mechanical only* answer) sorts every open wait into three kinds: what a typed check sees, what the host already witnesses (a precheck failing again, a person's press, a session's message), and judgment, which is work blocked on by id. The second kind has no check type yet: §4.2's *Later: `host-log`* names it and leaves it for a later step, and its consumer half (the host's own event log and its query) is shiftlead#TD-1295.

Until it exists, a consumer cannot write such a wait as a gate. Its entries keep the retired `**Waits:** event — …` line, which `check()` only notes (ℹ `retired`) and which no reader takes as a gate. A consumer's live check waiting on "the next message of this kind from that team" is one today. When the consumer stops reading `Waits` as a hold (its v2.4 adoption) or v2.5 retires `Waits`, those entries lose their hold and become pickable before the event happens.

**Ask:** Go on TD-045's revised design (`docs/plans/TD-045-host-log.md`, revision 2: log sources, the reader in agent-cadence): file B1 (`set` logs `gate added`, `gate_added()`) and B2 (the `host-log <source> [kind <k>] [times <N>] "<text>"` check, `event-undated`, `first_instant` and `count_host_log`), with agent-cadence's techlead filing the reader? Or One more round, or Hold with a note.

**Fix:** Designed in `docs/plans/TD-045-host-log.md` (review record `docs/reviews/TD-045.md`): a format change built in two builds, filed `Part of: TD-045` on Paul's go, in series. B1 makes `set` log `gate added` for each gate it adds, and adds `gate_added()` (the plan's *The builds*, rules 3 and 5's `set` half). B2 adds the `host-log <kind> [times <N>] "<text>"` check, `event-undated`, and `edit` re-dating a changed `host-log` check (rules 1, 2, 4, 5's `edit` half, 6 and 7). Each lands its `SPEC.md` text (the plan's *The `SPEC.md` text*) and its fixtures (*The fixture cases*). The findings no design review read (F81–F89) go into the builds' Fix. No release ships B1 without B2. Consumers: shiftlead#TD-1295's design gives each record an id and a repo, and its query counts by kind, literal text, repo and the day after `since`; agent-cadence's vendor PR for the release makes its event runner give no outcome for `host-log` and passes `today` to `set` and `edit`. Each is the techlead's to carry to that repo's own entry.
