## TD-132: An answer a delegate makes for the person can't be recorded as made for them

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 51ecef0c98fc4ea48d4e543bbf58c1a6
**Blocked by:** decision (paul)

**Why:** ShiftLead's director seat is to answer some cards on the person's behalf: merges within the person's merge line first, more kinds as their written preferences grow. That widening is ShiftLead's, filed from the person's conversation of 2026-11-16. Today a board answer records `Decided: … by <person>`, and the merge check accepts a gate's lift only from that gate's own person (or, on an `Answered by: any` board, anyone on `People:`), so a delegate has two choices, both wrong (putting the delegate on `People:` would make it a person, which it is not):
- It writes `by <person>`, and the record says the person decided something they did not. Later reviews, and the person, cannot tell their own decisions from the delegate's.
- It writes its own name, which no check accepts.

"A card answer is the person's answer" stays true only if a delegated answer says it was delegated.

**Ask:** Should the design letting a seat answer some questions for you get a twelfth review round, or be built now?

**Context:** One more round: a session runs a twelfth review round on round 11's fixes, which no design reviewer has read, then asks again. Go: a session files its one build and asks agent-cadence and ShiftLead to file theirs. Hold with a note: nothing is built, and a session revises the design as the note says.

**Design PR:** #406, #417, #421, #424, #427, #430

**Location:** SPEC.md (the board's Decided line, People); ledgerboard/__init__.py (the writers, the merge-gate verdicts)

**Fix:** Design, on long-term merit:
- **The record.** An answer can carry who made it for the person and under which authority, for example `Decided: 2026-11-20 by director for <person> (merge-line) — Merge`. The design settles the exact form.
- **Where delegations are declared.** Declare them where the checks can read them, perhaps beside `People:` on the board, for example `Delegates: director (merge-line, design-go-routine)`. The writers and the merge-gate verdicts accept a delegated answer only where a declared delegation covers that kind of answer, and refuse it elsewhere, mechanically.
- **How readers treat it.** Readers report a delegated answer as the person's for every purpose (gates lift, the Queue clears), and show who made it.
- **Release.** Decide whether this is a minor or a major release, and its order across agent-cadence and ShiftLead. Spec, code and fixtures land together.
- **Who may grant a delegation.** A delegate never grants itself authority. A change to the declared delegations is the person's own answer, or passes the person-paths gate, and the check refuses any other change.
- **Surfaces the design must cover:** both `Decided:` shapes (the v2 line's `(by …)` and the item file's `by … —`), and every place that hard-codes the board's preamble lines (`People`, `Answered by`, `Ledgerboard`). These are the split's carried-preamble handling and its equality check, so a new line is not dropped.

**Done when:** A design states the record form, where delegations are declared, and what the checks accept; its rounds converge, it gets the person's Go, and its builds are filed.
