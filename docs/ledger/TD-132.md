## TD-132: An answer a delegate makes for the person can't be recorded as made for them

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-17
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 51ecef0c98fc4ea48d4e543bbf58c1a6

**Why:** ShiftLead's director seat is to answer some cards on the person's behalf: merges within the person's merge line first, more kinds as their written preferences grow. That widening is ShiftLead's, filed from the person's conversation of 2026-11-16. Today a board answer records `Decided: … by <person>`, and the merge check accepts a gate's lift only from that gate's own person, so a delegate has two choices, both wrong:
- It writes `by <person>`, and the record says the person decided something they did not. Later reviews, and the person, cannot tell their own decisions from the delegate's.
- It writes its own name, which no check accepts.

"A card answer is the person's answer" stays true only if a delegated answer says it was delegated.

**Location:** SPEC.md (the board's Decided line, People); ledgerboard/__init__.py (the writers, the merge-gate verdicts)

**Fix:** Design, on long-term merit:
- **The record.** An answer can carry who made it for the person and under which authority, for example `Decided: 2026-11-20 by director for <person> (merge-line) — Merge`. The design settles the exact form.
- **Where delegations are declared.** Declare them where the checks can read them, perhaps beside `People:` on the board, for example `Delegates: director (merge-line, design-go-routine)`. The writers and the merge-gate verdicts accept a delegated answer only where a declared delegation covers that kind of answer, and refuse it elsewhere, mechanically.
- **How readers treat it.** Readers report a delegated answer as the person's for every purpose (gates lift, the Queue clears), and show who made it.
- **Release.** Decide whether this is a minor or a major release, and its order across agent-cadence and ShiftLead. Spec, code and fixtures land together.

**Done when:** A design states the record form, where delegations are declared, and what the checks accept; its rounds converge, it gets the person's Go, and its builds are filed.
