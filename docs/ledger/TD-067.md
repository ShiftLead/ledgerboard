## TD-067: A lift of one part of a shared decide item marks the whole item decided, for parts the person did not answer

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11 (ao-shiftlead-td-1551, through ao td add --repo; drafted by ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 1b5739ece28b43dc842f719111a75512
**Location:** ledgerboard/board.py (decide --board-only); ledgerboard/ledger.py (set --by --answer --shared-item); ledgerboard/__init__.py (closes, needs_you, covers, _answers_owing, lift_pr_verdict, tripwire, LOG_LIFT_SUFFIX); SPEC.md (The board: Closing on the answer, The gate sweep; Ledger entries and their edits; Versions); docs/plans/target-design.md (§1.1 the set by= route; §3.1 the shared-snapshot exception; §6 (b)); fixtures/board_writer/, fixtures/verdict/, fixtures/lift/, fixtures/board/

**Why:** The `set by=` route lifts a `decision (<person>)` gate on one entry: the consumer's wrapper runs `ledger.py set TD-n --blocked-by '' --by <person> --answer <label> --shared-item` (the Log line, ending `; shared board item left open`) and `board.py decide --board-only` (the board half) in one commit. When the covering `decide` item is a parent snapshot shared with other parts, the board half writes `Decided: <label> (by <person>)` on the shared item and `closes()` keeps it open, since another listed part still carries the gate. That is what the target design chose (`docs/plans/target-design.md` §1.1 *The writers live in ledgerboard*, the `set by=` route; §3.1 *Grammar, rules and lifting*, the shared-snapshot exception; §6 (b), `closes()`), and it says the remaining parts "are lifted by a re-answer through `board.py decide`". The harm: the item's one `Decided:` now speaks for parts the person did not answer.

- `needs_you()` classes any open item with a `Decided:` as `decided`, "a session's work order", ahead of the person's to-do. The parts still waiting on the person's answer drop out of what the person is asked, and the item reads as an order to act on them with that label.
- The answer cannot be given for one part only: the board line holds one `Decided:`, so it cannot say which part it was given for, and the wrapper refuses a label not in the item's `Lifts:` (else `Go`), so a free-text answer cannot stand in.
- The module's own gate sweep does **not** lift the other parts from it: `_answers_owing()` reads only a `closed-decided` item, and the close rule (§6) keeps the item open while any listed part carries the gate. A consumer that reads `decided` items as answers is the risk, and the person's missing question is the harm in any case.

Found by ShiftLead's session `ao-shiftlead-td-1551` (mail m-a4fa2e4d3431, about ShiftLead TD-1551, its PR #3447, 2026-11-11): its `decide` item had Context `TD-1551 decision (paul)` and Parts TD-1664, TD-1669; the person answered Go on TD-1664 alone; the lift of TD-1664 wrote `Decided: Go (by paul)` on the shared item, which still names TD-1669. It asked for a per-part lift that leaves the shared item undecided, or records the answer per part, and a test.

**Design PR:** #214

**Fix:** A design round first (Kind design-first): it reopens a shape the target design agreed, so what to change is the design's, and a release is Paul's. What the design settles:

- What a lift of one part of a shared item writes on the board (`board.py decide --board-only`): no `Decided:` (the item stays `open` and keeps asking for its other parts), an answer recorded per part, or the item split, and how each reads back through `parse_board()`, `needs_you()` and `covers()`.
- What the merge gate and the tripwire then accept for such a lift (§3.1's shared-snapshot exception, `lift_pr_verdict`, `tripwire`, the `; shared board item left open` Log suffix in `SPEC.md` *Ledger entries and their edits*), and how the remaining parts are later lifted.
- Whether it is a version event under `SPEC.md` *Versions*, and the `SPEC.md` text in *The board* (its *Closing on the answer* and *The gate sweep* paragraphs).
- Its fixtures: the existing shared-item cases (`fixtures/board_writer/decide_board_only_still_carried/`, `fixtures/verdict/cases/set by= on one part of a shared item, left open/`, `fixtures/lift/`) changed as the design says, and a new `fixtures/board/` case asserting the other parts stay asked of the person, plus a unit test.

Then the build(s) the design files, each landing its `SPEC.md` text with its code and fixtures; then a release (Paul's), agent-cadence's vendor PR and ShiftLead's sync PR.

**Done when:** A release of ledgerboard in which the `set by=` route's lift of one part of a `decide` item shared with other parts (`ledger.py set … --shared-item` with `board.py decide --board-only`) leaves the item asking the person for the parts still carrying the gate (`needs_you()` does not class it as a decided work order for them), with `SPEC.md`, fixtures and a test for it; the precheck green.
