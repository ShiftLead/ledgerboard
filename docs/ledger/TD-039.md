## TD-039: An archived blocker's unknown Status word releases its dependents, though §7.4 promises a newer minor never reads a block as lifted

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** ao-ledgerboard-lb-grinder
**Location:** SPEC.md Superseded and dropped; ledgerboard/__init__.py _ended()/_chain(); docs/plans/target-design.md §7.4

**Why:** Target design §7.4 (*Cross-version*) promises that a minor reader reading text from a newer minor of its major never reads a block as lifted. SPEC *Superseded and dropped* (v2.4) says the opposite for an archived blocker's Status: any word other than `Superseded … by` and `Dropped` "reads as closed and done, which releases the dependents". So a v2.4 reader that meets a Status word a later minor adds for a closed-but-not-done entry, say `Withdrawn 2026-01-05`, from another repo's resolver or its own archive, releases the dependent. Checked on main at 3b232c8: `pickable()` with a resolver answering `{"state": "archived", "status": "Withdrawn 2026-01-05"}` lists the blocked entry as pickable. Found while building TD-035's cross-version fixtures. TD-035 cannot carry a fixture for that half of the promise until this is decided.

**Fix:** Open in the design round: either (a) SPEC keeps "any other Status is closed and done", and §7.4's promise is narrowed to the words a reader knows, with the closed Status set (§2.2) making a new closed-not-done word a major change; or (b) the reader keeps the block on a Status word outside the words it knows (`Resolved`, `Done`, `Built`, `Designed`, `Superseded`, `Dropped`, none) and flags it, which is a v2.x behaviour change and needs a Versions line. Then SPEC, the module and a cross-version fixture agree.

**Done when:** SPEC *Superseded and dropped* and target design §7.4 say the same thing about an unknown archived Status word, and a fixture shows it.
