## TD-078: v3.0's ledger half: stop reading v2's old forms, remove migrate, and give an archived entry a writer route

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-12
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open

**Why:** v3.0 has two halves (target design §7.1's v3.0 row). TD-070 designs the board's: the board as a directory. The ledger's half has no entry: v3.0 stops reading what v2 kept only for old files — `Waits`, `Taken by`, the old `decision (who) — text` form, a `Resolved` header field — and removes `migrate` with them. TD-070's plan names this half as "§7.1's and another entry's"; this is that entry. Paul's Go on TD-070's scope (2026-11-11) asked for it to be filed.

An archived entry has no writer route today: `note`, `set` and `edit` take only open entries, so an old form left in an archived entry can be rewritten only by hand, which the cadence forbids. Met on 2026-11-11: PR #226 first named two archived entries (TD-067, TD-073) in its title, the merge gate's ledger row asked it to touch their files, which no writer can, and the PR was retitled instead; its review also left an archived entry's stale `(v2.8)` Log text as it was, for the same reason. §7.1's precondition for v3.0 counts archived entries too, so this half must give them a route before v3.0 can be cut.

**Design PR:** #243

**Location:** SPEC.md (Retired words, Migrating to v2.7, Versions); ledgerboard/ledger.py; docs/plans/target-design.md §7.1

**Fix:** Design first, against §7.1's v3.0 row, beside TD-070 (one v3.0 release):
- What v3.0's reader stops reading, and what it does on meeting each old form (an error, a finding, or a refusal), for open and archived entries alike.
- A writer route for an archived entry: at least a dated Log line (`note`) and the rewrite of an old form, so each repo can meet the precondition with no hand edit. Whether that route ships in a v2.x minor first, so repos can clean their archives before v3.0.
- `migrate`'s removal, and the check each consumer's v3.0 vendor PR runs first (`migrate --plan` shows no changes; no entry, open or archived, holds an old form).
- The order with TD-070's builds, and the SPEC text and fixtures each build lands.

**Done when:** A design, reviewed and agreed, that names v3.0's ledger removals, the archived-entry writer route and its release, and the precondition check, with its builds filed; TD-070 and this entry together cover §7.1's v3.0 row.
