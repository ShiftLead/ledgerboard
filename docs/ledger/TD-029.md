## TD-029: Answering a decision doesn't lift it: no module function writes the lift, so each route would write its own

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** 2e9dce7d0dea
**Blocked by:** TD-011
**Part of:** TD-010
**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py (lift_gate, decide_lift, closes, edit_line decide); SPEC.md The board, The ledger; fixtures/ledger_edit, fixtures/edit

**Why:** A decided entry stays blocked until someone edits it by hand (TD-010's Why). Every route that lifts a gate (the decide lift, `set by=`, a host's event and `until` lifts, the cross-repo lift) must write the same text, and the merge gate must be able to recompute it (target design §1.1, §6, §6.2).

**Fix:** The module half of lifting, per target design §1.1 (*One action lifts a gate*), §1.2 (the sweep's functions' shared parts), §3.1, §6 and §6.2:
- `lift_gate(text, gate, by, answer=…, evidence=…, suffix=…, today)`: removes the gate and writes the matching fixed `gate lifted` shape; refuses inputs that don't match a shape (§1.1's matrix) and a `by` that may not lift;
- `decide_lift(item_line, entries, label, by, today, board_people, answered_by)`: the whole decide lift over the Context entry and its `Parts:` snapshot (lifted, skipped, kept waiting; emptied `Blocked by` removed; refusals for an archived or missing entry, a gate person off `People:`, an answerer not allowed); event items answered `Drop the gate` or `Keep waiting`;
- `Lifts:`'s semantics (labels compared case-insensitively, `Go` when absent; `item_line(lifts=)` and `item_problem()` refuse a waiting-only label);
- `waiting_only(label)` (`Keep waiting`, `Not yet`), `closes(item, label, entries_after)`, `parse_closed_items(content)`;
- `edit_line`'s `decide` gains `close=` (`Closed: D — kept waiting.` / `— lifted.`), with read-backs;
- the `Answered by: owner | any` preamble line and `answered_by(content)`;
- reassign (`decision (a)` → `decision (b)`, refusing b = a, ℹ past two reassigns) and the override lift (`override: <why>`, a why required);
- a fixture shows `lift_gate`'s output equals `decide_lift`'s for each shape.

**Done when:** The lift functions are built with SPEC text and §7.4's *decide_lift*, *Parent go* and *Reassign* fixtures, and the suite passes.
