**Why:** two board writes have no writer in either consumer: a new `decide` item, with its kind, `Answers:`, `(default)` mark and detail block, and the archive of closed items. Both are typed by hand. The other writes go through this module: agent-cadence's `board_edit.py` makes snooze, done, decide and reply through `edit_line()`, and ShiftLead's `board.py` adds plain and decided items through `item_line()` and `decided_line()` as well. But `item_line()` takes no kind or answers, `decided_line()` writes only an already-decided line, and nothing composes a detail block or archives. Each hand-typed convention is one an agent can slip on, the way the ledger's did before TD-005. The ledger's fix was one writer and a gate that refuses what the writer would not have written; the board needs the same, and its format half belongs here, since both consumers vendor this module.

**Ask:** Should TD-008's board-writer design go to its builds as planned (Go), get one more review round, or be held with a note?

**Fix:** designed in [the plan](../plans/TD-008-board-writers.md), a minor release (v2.3), three builds in series:
- B1, §1: `item_line()` writes every kind, with `kind`, `answers`, `default` and a `detail` block made from named parts. It re-reads what it writes, and it no longer writes the three wrong lines v2.2 writes.
- B2, §2: `item_problem()`, `canonical_item()` (which never changes a line's item key) and `board_problems()`, the gate, which judges only new lines. B1 must land first.
- B3, §3: `archive_closed()` and `archive_subject()`, and the *Versions* paragraph. B2 must land first.

Each build is filed with `**Part of:** TD-008` on the person's go, and names its plan section. Consumers, each filed on its own ledger and never from here: agent-cadence's `board.py` entry (the CLI, `fmt`, the gate, and the seed's `Format:` line), then ShiftLead's vendor PR.
