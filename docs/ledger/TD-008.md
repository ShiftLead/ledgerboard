## TD-008: A new decide item and an archive of closed ones have no writer: the board's writers cover every write, as the ledger's now do

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-30 (Paul, session `led-tds`: "board.py with alias, item line only, fmt pass, wait")
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open 2026-10-30: shape agreed in conversation; design rounds not run. 2026-10-31: Paul's go (board, 2026-10-30) lifts his block, and agent-cadence#TD-202 is archived; Paul named the ledgerboard team to build it. 2026-10-31: Paul routed it to the designer (td flow): the design's review rounds run before its builds are filed.
**Taken by:** 965518f644df
**Location:** `ledgerboard.py` (`item_line`, a new `item_problem` and canonical item writer, a new `archive_closed`); SPEC.md *The board* and *Edits*; `fixtures/board/`, `fixtures/edit/`

**Why:** two board writes have no writer in either consumer: a new `decide` item, with its kind, `Answers:`, `(default)` mark and detail block, and the archive of closed items. Both are typed by hand. The other writes go through this module: agent-cadence's `board_edit.py` makes snooze, done, decide and reply through `edit_line()`, and ShiftLead's `board.py` adds plain and decided items through `item_line()` and `decided_line()` as well. But `item_line()` takes no kind or answers, `decided_line()` writes only an already-decided line, and nothing composes a detail block or archives. Each hand-typed convention is one an agent can slip on, the way the ledger's did before TD-005. The ledger's fix was one writer and a gate that refuses what the writer would not have written; the board needs the same, and its format half belongs here, since both consumers vendor this module.

**Ask:** answered 2026-10-30 — Paul's go ahead (board); 2026-10-31 he named the ledgerboard team to build it.

**Fix:** the shape Paul agreed on 2026-10-30, a minor release (v2.3):
- `item_line()` gains `kind`, `answers` and `default`, so a new `decide` item is written whole; the detail block is composed from named parts (Context, Question, Caveats, Recommended) and never edited by a writer afterwards.
- `item_problem(line)`: why an open item line is not one the writers write, or None; and a canonical item writer that rewrites a line to that form or refuses one it would have to lose text from, for agent-cadence's one-time `fmt` pass over every board.
- `archive_closed(board, archive)`: the closed items moved as whole entries, detail blocks with them.
- The gate checks the item line only; the detail block stays the person's prose.

**Done when:** the board writers are built: v2.3's `item_line()` with kind and answers, `item_problem` and the canonical item writer, and `archive_closed`, with SPEC and fixtures, merged; then Paul's release, and agent-cadence's `board.py` vendoring it (its own entry).
