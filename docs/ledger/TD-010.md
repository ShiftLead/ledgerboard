## TD-010: Five ways to hold an entry that the tools read differently: a held entry gets picked, and an answered decision stays blocked

**Priority:** High
**Added:** 2026-10-31
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** 2e9dce7d0dea
**Blocked by:** decision (Paul) — the design's review rounds, then Paul's go
**Location:** ledgerboard.py (pickable, check, the Blocked by parser, ENTRY_ORDER); SPEC.md The ledger; fixtures/ledger

**Why:** an entry can be kept from being picked in five ways, and the tools that read them disagree. `pickable()` reads only `Blocked by` (open ids, `decision (<who>)`) and open parts. ShiftLead's **Hold** is stored in its home's settle clock (`ledger_seen.json`), not in the ledger, so an agent-cadence session, or ShiftLead on another machine, picks an entry the person held; that breaks cadence.md §3.6's own rule that a hold holds once merged. `**Waits:**` (a date or an event) is honoured by ShiftLead and ignored by `ledger.py --pickable`, so the two consumers disagree on what is pickable. A Status saying "held" is read by nothing. And a hold is often written as `decision (<who>)` (the layer rule that holds a design's builds, a design round's `Hold: <note>` answer), so the person sees a "decision" that asks nothing. Separately, answering a decision does not lift it: `board_edit.py decide` and ShiftLead's `decided_line()` write the board only, so a decided entry stays blocked until a session edits it by hand (TD-008 sat a day that way, 2026-10-30).

**Ask:** Is the gate set decision / hold / until / event, with Waits retired into Blocked by, the shape to run review rounds on, and once they converge, go?

**Fix:** the shape Paul agreed on 2026-10-31, a format change (v2.3 or later), then each consumer's vendor PR:
- `Blocked by` becomes the one place for anything that keeps an entry from being picked. After its ids it takes a small closed set of **gates**: `decision (<who>)` (the person owes an answer), `hold (<who>)` (the person said not now and owes nothing), `until YYYY-MM-DD` (a date) and `event (<what>)` (something outside the ledger). `pickable()` treats each as blocking; `until` lifts on the caller's date, so `pickable()` takes `today`.
- **`Waits:` is retired into `Blocked by`**: it leaves the canonical order, `check()` flags it, and a one-time rewrite moves each `Waits:` line into its entry's `Blocked by` as `until` or `event`.
- ShiftLead's Hold and Release write `hold (<who>)` to the entry through `edit_entry`, in a tool PR as a rank write is; its settle clock keeps only settling.
- The decide writer closes the loop: deciding a `decide` item whose `Context:` names TD-n also drops `decision (<who>)` from TD-n, in the same commit.
- A design round's `Hold: <note>` answer writes `hold (<who>)`, never `decision`.
- Open in the design round: the exact spelling of the gates and of a gate's pointer; whether `hold` carries a why; how the `decide` writer reaches an entry in another repo.

**Done when:** a release whose `pickable()` reads every gate, with SPEC and fixtures; no `Waits:` left in this ledger; and consumer entries filed in agent-cadence (`ledger.py`, `board_edit.py decide`, the layer and §4.8 wording) and ShiftLead (Hold/Release, `decided_line`).
