## TD-148: A v1 entry ending in its Ask is flagged when an entry separator follows it

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-18 (agent-cadence's grinder, through an entry request; drafted by ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead)
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 2f6c6f691ac24e818f4b9160467ee24d
**Part of:** TD-124

**Why:** v3.3's `field-lines` check flags an entry in a v1 ledger whose last section is its `**Ask:**` line, because the `---` line that separates it from the next entry is read as text past the Ask's line. agent-cadence's v1 seed template (its `files/docs/technical_debt.md`) separates entries with `---`, so every consumer ledger written from that seed whose entry ends in an Ask gets the flag. Migrating cannot cure it: `migrate-body` would move the `---` under a `**Context:**` lead, and the writer refuses edits to the flagged entry. Reproduced on main: `_body_faults("**Why:** x.\n\n**Ask:** Should it? Yes or no.\n\n---\n\n")` returns `[('field-lines', 'ask', 'lines', 2)]`; with the `---` before the Ask instead, nothing is flagged. Found by agent-cadence's grinder while vendoring v3.3 (agent-cadence TD-283). TD-124's design (D3, D6) does not mention a thematic break.

**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py (_body_faults, _ask_reading, migrate_body); SPEC.md (The body's shape, data fields)

**Fix:** Settle the rule, then build it with SPEC text and fixtures in one PR (a minor release, since `check()` flags less). The asker suggests a thematic break line (`---`, `***`, `___`) ends an Ask's section, or the body, in a v1 ledger. Open for the design: whether the break ends any data field's section (`Ask`, and `Answers` and `Lifts` where read) in every version, or only in v1, where it is the separator between entries; or whether a v1 entry's trailing separator is instead not part of its body at all (the reader and `split()` then agree on it); and what `migrate-body` does with the break (leave it in place, never under a `**Context:**` lead).

**Done when:** One PR has merged with the SPEC text, fixtures showing a v1 entry ending in an Ask and a `---` separator unflagged and left alone by `migrate-body`, and the suite, the fixtures and ruff green.
