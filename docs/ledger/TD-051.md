## TD-051: `board.py decide` refuses a parent's answer when a listed part was archived, where SPEC skips it

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-04
**Owner:** designer
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Open 2026-11-05: Designed (docs/plans/TD-051-archived-parts.md); rounds ended by the stop rule after round 1; waits on Paul's go
**Taken by:** ao-ledgerboard-lb-designer
**Blocked by:** decision (Paul)
**Location:** ledgerboard/board.py (`_decide_ledger`), ledgerboard/__init__.py (`decide_lift`, `closes`, `lift_pr_verdict`), SPEC.md *The decide lift* and *The board writer*, fixtures/lift, fixtures/board_writer, fixtures/verdict
**Related:** TD-050

**Why:** `board.py decide` on a `decide` whose `Parts:` lists a part since moved to the ledger's archive refuses the whole answer: `_decide_ledger` hands `decide_lift()` open entries alone, so the archived part reads as missing ("a part the item lists, is not among the entries given"). SPEC *The decide lift* says a listed part that no longer carries the gate is skipped and logged (`gate lift skipped`), and an archived part carries nothing, so the person is left a question no press can answer. Found while designing TD-050 (docs/plans/TD-050-parts-writer.md, *Why the check is at write time*). Its reach is wider than `decide`: once `_decide_ledger` hands `decide_lift()` archive-directory texts, `closes()` (which reads `_carried()` on the texts it is handed) would read one whose Status reads `Open` as still carrying the gate and hold the item open after a skip; and `lift_pr_verdict()` already hands `decide_lift()` and `closes()` such texts (`b_closed`), so the verdict and the writer disagree today.

**Ask:** Go to file TD-051's one build (B1: `decide_lift(archived=)` through `board.py decide` and `lift_pr_verdict()`), or buy one more review round for F8-F15.

**Fix:** Designed in docs/plans/TD-051-archived-parts.md (D1–D6). Option A: `decide_lift()` gains `archived=()`, the ids the caller read from the archive (D2). `board.py decide` passes the `archive/` ids there, never their texts (D4). `lift_pr_verdict()` splits the base ledger by directory, not Status, and passes the same (D5). `closes()` is unchanged; SPEC states its callers' contract (D3). The sweep's Status reading of archived texts is out of scope (D6). The SPEC text is the plan's *The `SPEC.md` text* 1–5, and the fixtures are its *Fixtures*. One build, B1, filed `Part of: TD-051` on Paul's go. No consumer entry: a consumer that calls `decide_lift()` itself gains the skip by passing `archived`.

**Done when:** B1 merged: `board.py decide` answers a parent's item whose listed part was archived (`decide_parent_snapshot_part_archived` and its `_open` twin). The part is skipped and logged, the others are lifted, and the item is closed per `closes()`. `lift_pr_verdict()` carves out the matching PR. The plan's `lift`, `board_writer` and `verdict` fixtures pass, and SPEC's text 1–5 from the plan landed with the code.
