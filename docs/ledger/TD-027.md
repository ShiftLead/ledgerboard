## TD-027: `Blocked by` holds only ids and one free-text decision form, so a date or an outside event can't block an entry the module reads

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** 2e9dce7d0dea
**Blocked by:** TD-026
**Part of:** TD-010
**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py (the Blocked by parser, pickable, check, ENTRY_ORDER); SPEC.md The ledger, Appendix: grammar; fixtures/ledger

**Why:** Five ways to hold an entry are read differently by the two consumers (TD-010). The agreed fix is one closed set of gates in `Blocked by` that `pickable()` reads (target design §3.1).

**Fix:** Per target design §2.1, §2.4 and §3.1:
- the grammar: ids, then gates `decision (<person>)`, `until YYYY-MM-DD`, `event (<name>)`, rendered in that order; at most one decision per person, one `until`, differently named events; the person grammar;
- `pickable(…, *, today)` with `today` required (the UTC date): an entry is pickable when every id is archived and it has no decision, no event, and no `until` after today; callers fixed in each vendor PR;
- the old form `decision (who) — text`: read only as the last item; `new` and `set` refuse it; a writer refuses to add any gate to an entry that still has it;
- retired words (§2.1's reserved list: `Waits`, `Taken by`, `Resolved` as a field, `hold`, a role in `decision (…)`, the stage words) named *retired* with their replacement by `check()`; a retired gate keeps the block;
- `**Ask:**` required with a `decision`; `check()` notes (ℹ) a past `until`;
- the Priority check (High, Medium or Low: an error on a changed entry, ℹ on a standing ledger);
- `parts_gates` on a parent's record (derived, direct parts, canonical keys);
- `Waits` kept in canonical order but never read as a gate;
- SPEC's grammar appendix adds `Blocked by`; SPEC states that a part never inherits its parent's gates.

**Done when:** The gates, `pickable(…, today)` and the checks are built with SPEC text and fixtures (the old-form guard, each retired word, `parts_gates`), and the suite passes.
