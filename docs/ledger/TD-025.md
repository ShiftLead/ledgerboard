## TD-025: An entry's history is free prose in its Status, so no tool can read when a gate was added, lifted or kept waiting

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** 2e9dce7d0dea
**Part of:** TD-010
**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py (entry writers, check); SPEC.md The ledger; fixtures/ledger, fixtures/ledger_edit

**Why:** Lifts, the gate sweep and the decide lift all need dated, machine-readable history: a gate's age, who lifted it and on what evidence. Today `note`, `archive` and `reopen` append prose to Status, which nothing can parse (target design §2.2).

**Fix:** The Log, per target design §2.2:
- the `**Log:**` section: dated bullet lines, oldest first, always the body's last section (`check()` flags a section after it; writers refuse to append to a Log that isn't last; `edit` and `archive` insert before it), one line per bullet, created by the first writer that touches an entry without one;
- the writer-made shapes (`filed by`, `gate added:`, `gate lifted:` in its five shapes and three suffixes, `gate lift skipped:`, `kept waiting:`, `gate reassigned:`, `archived:`, `reopened —`, `was:`), parsed by the module, with the reserved prefixes `note` refuses and the quoting rule (a quoted label runs to the line's last `"`);
- from v2.4, `new`, `note`, `archive` and `reopen` write the Log line only: Status becomes `<word> <date>` (bare `Open`) and `Added` a bare date; `new` writes one `gate added` line per gate it files with;
- `status_notes_to_log(entry_text)`, the one pure function that lifts a Status note into Log lines (its grammar, `— was:` cut, no split inside a tail, insertion in date order, reserved-prefix escaping); `archive`, `reopen` and `note` call it before writing; a Status it can't read is copied whole into a `was:` line by `archive` and `reopen`;
- a gate's age, one definition (the latest `gate added`, `kept waiting` or `gate reassigned` line, else the first Log line, else `Added`);
- `rename_ref` rewrites a `while` token, never other Log prose;
- every ledger date is UTC: the entry writers turn an aware datetime into its UTC date and refuse a naive one (§3.1).

**Done when:** The Log's shapes, writers and `status_notes_to_log` are built with SPEC text and fixtures (each shape parsed; a reserved-prefix note refused; an unknown Log prefix kept byte for byte; a naive datetime refused), and the suite passes.
