## TD-010: Five ways to hold an entry that the tools read differently: a held entry gets picked, and an answered decision stays blocked

**Priority:** High
**Added:** 2026-10-31
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-04

**Why:** an entry can be kept from being picked in five ways, and the tools that read them disagree. `pickable()` reads only `Blocked by` (open ids, `decision (<who>)`) and open parts. ShiftLead's **Hold** is stored in its home's settle clock (`ledger_seen.json`), not in the ledger, so an agent-cadence session, or ShiftLead on another machine, picks an entry the person held; that breaks cadence.md §3.6's own rule that a hold holds once merged. `**Waits:**` (a date or an event) is honoured by ShiftLead and ignored by `ledger.py --pickable`, so the two consumers disagree on what is pickable. A Status saying "held" is read by nothing. And a hold is often written as `decision (<who>)` (the layer rule that holds a design's builds, a design round's `Hold: <note>` answer), so the person sees a "decision" that asks nothing. Separately, answering a decision does not lift it: `board_edit.py decide` and ShiftLead's `decided_line()` write the board only, so a decided entry stays blocked until a session edits it by hand (TD-008 sat a day that way, 2026-10-30).

**Location:** ledgerboard (its parts, TD-025 to TD-037); docs/plans/target-design.md
