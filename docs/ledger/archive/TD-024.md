## TD-024: item_problem() passes a line with a tab or other non-space whitespace after its `- [ ] `

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-03

**Why:** SPEC's *The form the writers write* says an item line opens `- [ ] ` at column 0, single-spaced, and `item_problem()` checks only `line.startswith("- [ ] ")` and not `"- [ ]  "`. So a line such as `- [ ] <tab>act 2026-01-05 (n/a) — x. Context: TD-7. Due: 2026-01-20.` (a tab, `\r`, `\x0b` or U+2028 after the box) passes the gate, though no writer writes it: `ITEM_RE`'s `\s+` and the rebuilt `want` both drop that whitespace.
