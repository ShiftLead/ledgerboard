## TD-039: An archived blocker's unknown Status word releases its dependents, though §7.4 promises a newer minor never reads a block as lifted

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-03
**Kind:** design-first
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-04: its one build, TD-043 (PR #129), keeps an archived blocker's unknown Status word from releasing its dependents, as the plan designed
**Taken by:** ao-ledgerboard-lb-grinder
**Location:** docs/plans/TD-039-unknown-status.md

**Why:** Target design §7.4 (*Cross-version*) promises that a minor reader reading text from a newer minor of its major never reads a block as lifted. SPEC *Superseded and dropped* (v2.4) says the opposite for an archived blocker's Status: any word other than `Superseded … by` and `Dropped` "reads as closed and done, which releases the dependents". So a v2.4 reader that meets a Status word a later minor adds for a closed-but-not-done entry, say `Withdrawn 2026-01-05`, from another repo's resolver or its own archive, releases the dependent. Checked on main at 3b232c8: `pickable()` with a resolver answering `{"state": "archived", "status": "Withdrawn 2026-01-05"}` lists the blocked entry as pickable. Found while building TD-035's cross-version fixtures. The design itself leans both ways: its Mixed-version row (§0.4 prior-art table, row 9) says only that an older reader reads a newer *closed* Status as closed, which supports keeping SPEC's rule. TD-035 cannot carry a fixture for that half of the promise until this is decided.
