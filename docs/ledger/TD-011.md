## TD-011: A superseded blocker releases the entries waiting on it before the entry that replaced it lands

**Priority:** Medium
**Added:** 2026-10-31
**Status:** Open 2026-11-02: Shaped in TD-010's round (target design §5: supersede rewires, drop refuses while dependents remain); Paul's go 2026-11-02 lifts his gate; built with v2.4, after TD-008 2026-11-03: Blocked by the v2.3 release (TD-021) rather than TD-008, which archives with TD-019 before the release 2026-11-03: v2.3 is released (TD-021 archived), so its Blocked by goes; it waits for the v2.4 builds the designer files from target design §7.1 (§7.0), since its own Fix is still 'to be shaped' and §5 is marked proposed 2026-11-03: Rescoped from target design §5 as v2.4's fifth build, after TD-028 (events) in the series of TD-010's parts

**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py (pickable, check, edit_entry rename_ref, the resolver); SPEC.md The archived entry; fixtures/ledger

**Why:** `pickable()` lifts a blocker once its heading is in the archive and never reads how it got there. An entry archived `Superseded … by TD-m` therefore releases every entry blocked by it at once, though its requirement now lives in TD-m, which is still open. cadence.md §2.8 has the superseded entry's remaining asks written into TD-m first, but nothing moves its dependents' `Blocked by` to TD-m. `edit_entry`'s `rename_ref` exists for that and nothing calls it on a supersede. Example: TD-10 is blocked by TD-5; TD-5 is superseded by TD-12; TD-10 is picked and built on a design that lives, unfinished, in TD-12.

**Done when:** The rules are built with SPEC text and §7.4's *Supersede edge cases* fixtures (a chain, dedupe, a cycle, a chain ending in Dropped, a cross-repo successor), and the suite passes.

**Ask:** Answered in TD-010's round (target design §5, Paul's go 2026-11-02): a supersede rewires its dependents; a drop refuses while they remain.

**Fix:** Per target design §5 (shaped in TD-010's round; Paul's go 2026-11-02):
- under v2.4, `pickable()` and `check()` read an archived Status that starts with `Superseded` or `Dropped` by the rules below; every other archived Status reads as closed and done;
- an open entry blocked by a superseded or dropped entry is an error on edits and ℹ on a standing ledger;
- `archive --superseded-by TD-m` renames the old id to TD-m in every open dependent's `Blocked by`, in the same change (`rename_ref`); a duplicate is `Superseded D by` the original;
- `archive --dropped` refuses while any open entry depends on the dropped one, listing every dependent;
- the edge cases: the successor must exist and not be Dropped; a chain is followed to its end; dedupe; a cycle refused; a chain ending in Dropped refused;
- across repos: the resolver returns an archived entry's Status word and successor id as well as its state, so `check()` and `pickable()` apply the rules to `other#TD-m`; a cross-repo dependent is reported (ℹ) until the gate sweep rewires it.
