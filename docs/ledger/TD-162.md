## TD-162: The ledger writer cannot clear a question's answers once its decision is gone

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23 (shiftlead's grinder, by request; drafted by lb-techlead)
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 3d7a3eb2c9ea47ee8ac477c8e3e0b462

**Why:** The ledger writer can remove an entry's Ask once its decision is gone, but it refuses the `**Answers:**`, `**Lifts:**` and `**Context:**` that belong to that question. ShiftLead's design §4.9c (reconcile, an entry whose Team line names another team) has its home clear the decision, Ask and Answers through this writer. The Answers cannot be removed today, and the design does not name the Lifts and Context, which the home also writes for the question and which would stay behind. `entry_answers()` reads these sections whatever the gate, so a decision added later offers the old question's answers and lift under a new Ask, and `check()` flags nothing because they are well formed. Reproduced on an invented entry at 6f4b554: `edit --section Answers --remove` (and Lifts, Context) is refused on an entry with no decision, and an entry given a new decision and Ask keeps the old Context, Answers and Lifts with no flag.

**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py (edit_entry, remove); ledgerboard/ledger.py (edit --remove); SPEC.md (The ledger, Ledger entries and their edits, The ledger writer, Versions); fixtures/ledger_edit/cases.json

**Fix:** In `edit_entry()`'s `remove` branch, let the v3.1 rule that takes the Ask of an entry with no decision also take `Answers`, `Lifts` and `Context`, with the same refusal while `_names_decision()` holds and when the section is missing. Each section is removed by its own write, as now, and any order is safe. Update the `--remove` help in `ledger.py`, the refusal's wording, and the SPEC passages that state the Ask-only rule: *The ledger* (the cure for an Ask with no decision), *Ledger entries and their edits*, *The ledger writer* (`edit … --remove`), and *Versions* (a new version's paragraph beside v3.1's). Add cases beside the `edit_remove_*` ones in `fixtures/ledger_edit/cases.json`. A release and the consumers' vendor PRs follow, as for any format change.

**Done when:** On an entry whose Blocked by names no decision, `edit --section NAME --remove` removes `Answers`, `Lifts` or `Context`, and `entry_answers()` then reads None once Answers and Lifts are gone. The same removal is refused while a decision (or a retired item standing for one) holds, and for a section the entry lacks. Fixtures cover each of the three sections in both cases, and SPEC says so.
