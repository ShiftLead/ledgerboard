## TD-162: The ledger writer cannot clear a question's answers once its decision is gone

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-23 (shiftlead's grinder, by request; drafted by lb-techlead)
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 3d7a3eb2c9ea47ee8ac477c8e3e0b462

**Why:** The ledger writer can remove an entry's Ask once its decision is gone, but it refuses the `**Answers:**`, `**Lifts:**` and `**Context:**` that belong to that question. ShiftLead's home has to clear a question from an entry moved to another team (its design §4.9c, reconcile; its TD-2051), so those three sections stay behind. `entry_answers()` reads them whatever the gate, so a decision added later offers the old question's answers and lift under a new Ask, and nothing flags it because they are well formed. Reproduced on an invented entry at 6f4b554: `edit --section Answers --remove` (and Lifts, Context) is refused on an entry with no decision, and an entry given a new decision and Ask still returns the old answers.

**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py (edit_entry, remove); ledgerboard/ledger.py (edit --remove); SPEC.md (Edits, Versions); fixtures/ledger_edit/

**Fix:** In `edit_entry()`'s `remove` branch, let the v3.1 rule that takes the Ask of an entry with no decision also take `Answers`, `Lifts` and `Context`, with the same refusal while `_names_decision()` holds and when the section is missing. Each section is removed by its own write, as now. Update the `--remove` help in `ledger.py`, the refusal's wording, SPEC *Edits* and *Versions*, and add fixtures under `fixtures/ledger_edit/`. A release and the consumers' vendor PRs follow, as for any format change.

**Done when:** On an entry whose Blocked by names no decision, `edit --section NAME --remove` removes `Answers`, `Lifts` or `Context`, and `entry_answers()` then reads None once Answers and Lifts are gone. The same removal is refused while a decision (or a retired item standing for one) holds, and for a section the entry lacks. Fixtures cover each of the three sections in both cases, and SPEC says so.
