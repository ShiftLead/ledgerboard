## TD-053: `sweep()` asks again a decision already answered with a lift when its item was closed `— lifted.` but the gate still stands

**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py (`sweep()`'s `decision` branch and `may_file()`, `covers()`/`_covers()`, `_answered_within()`), SPEC.md *The gate sweep* (the `decision (<p>)` bullet and *One filing rule*), fixtures/sweep, tests/

**Why:** A `decide` item that a person answered with a lift, but that was closed `— lifted.` while its gate still stands on the entry, is invisible to the sweep. `covers()` counts only `open` and `decided` items, not `closed-decided` ones. `_answered_within()` counts only answers that do not lift. This is by design: SPEC's `decision (<p>)` bullet counts only an item "answered without a lift", and target design §1.2 says "an item closed `— lifted.` settled the old question and opens no window, so a gate re-added later is asked at once" (fixture `sweep` TD-211: lifted, the gate re-added ten days later, the sweep files). On the board, the case here looks the same as TD-211's. Only the entry's Log tells them apart: a `gate lifted` line for the gate on or after the item's `Decided:` date means the gate was lifted and then re-added, and no such line means it was never lifted. So the next sweep files a fresh `Go | Not yet` item for each entry still carrying the gate, asking the person again what they already answered. Since v2.4, `closes()` closes a lifting answer only once nothing listed carries the gate, so the state arises only when the item was closed some other way: an answer written before `decide_lift()` existed, a hand edit, or a write that closed the item and then failed to lift. It happened live in ShiftLead on 2026-11-05. Paul's Go on its TD-1392 closed the decide line `lifted` before the decide lift existed, so `decision (paul)` stayed on TD-1392 and its four builds. ShiftLead's in-process sweep, which calls the vendored `sweep()`, then filed five new `Go | Not yet` items. agent-cadence's `gate_sweep.py` calls the same `sweep()`, so it re-asks the same way. The design (target design §1.2's close rule, §4.4) has a host-filed `act` for a lift PR closed unmerged (`Context: TD-n <gate> lift`), but nothing for a lifting answer closed with no lift.

**Ask:** Go to file TD-053's one build (B1: `sweep()` files an `act` saying a closed lifting answer's lift is owed, in place of re-asking `Go | Not yet`): round 8 read the whole plan, round 7's fixes F66-F71 first, and found nothing, so the rounds ended by the stop rule; or buy one more round.

**Fix:** Designed in [docs/plans/TD-053-unlifted-answer.md](../plans/TD-053-unlifted-answer.md) (review record docs/reviews/TD-053.md): option (a). A closed lifting answer whose gate the entry's Log shows was never lifted (no `gate lifted` line on or after its date less one day, no `gate added` on or after its date plus two) owes the lift; the answers owing for one Context entry and gate, across the boards read, file one `act` (`<gate> on TD-n was answered with a lift on <D> but still stands on <ids>: lift it.`, Context `TD-n <gate> lift`) once per answer within `reask_days`, in place of `Go | Not yet`; the entries owed are held, and the act closes once nothing is owed. A gate lifted and re-added (TD-211) is asked at once, as before. It ships in v2.6 (plan D1). One build, filed on Paul's go with `**Part of:** TD-053`: B1, `sweep()`'s pre-pass and close rule, SPEC texts 1–4 in *The gate sweep* and *Versions*, the `fixtures/sweep` cases. Consumers need only their vendor PRs; ShiftLead's own half is its TD-1425.
