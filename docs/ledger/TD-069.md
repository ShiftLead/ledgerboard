## TD-069: A detail block's Recommended reads its default's label twice when the caller's text already opens with it

**Priority:** Low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-11 (ao-shiftlead-td-1551, through ao td add --repo; drafted by ao-ledgerboard-lb-techlead)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 92a863ea9697436db917025aafd1c76f
**Location:** ledgerboard/__init__.py (_detail_lines, CONSEQUENCE_SEP); ledgerboard/board.py (add --detail help); SPEC.md (The board writer, the detail block); fixtures/board_writer/; tests/

**Why:** On a `decide` with a default, the detail block's Recommended bullet opens with the default answer's label and ` — ` (`SPEC.md` *The board writer*, the detail block; `_detail_lines()` in `ledgerboard/__init__.py`, which prefixes `label` and `CONSEQUENCE_SEP` to the `recommended` words). A caller that writes its `recommended` text starting with that label, which reads naturally, gets the label twice: `**Recommended:** Go — Go — …`. Nothing refuses or warns, and `board.py add --help` says only that `--detail` is "the detail block's parts, a JSON object", so a caller who has not read SPEC cannot know. No writer touches the block afterwards, so the doubled words stay in front of the person until the item closes.

Found by ShiftLead's session `ao-shiftlead-td-1551` (mail m-fe30ed35268f, 2026-11-11): a board item it added showed `**Recommended:** Go — Go — TD-1664 has drawn…`. It asked for `--help` to say the label is added, or for `add` to strip or refuse a leading `<label> —`, with a test.

**Fix:** A build. In `_detail_lines()`, refuse a `recommended` whose first words are the default's label followed by ` — ` (whitespace collapsed, the label compared as the answers compare it), naming the prefix the writer adds. A refusal, not a silent strip: the writers refuse what would not read as the caller meant (`SPEC.md` *Edits*), and stripping would change the caller's words without telling them. Say the prefix in `board.py`'s `--detail` help and in `SPEC.md` *The board writer*'s detail-block sentence, beside the refusal. Fixtures: a refused case in `fixtures/board_writer/` (or `fixtures/edit/`, where `item_line(detail=)` is run), and a unit test in `tests/`.

**Done when:** `item_line()` and `board.py add` refuse a `recommended` that opens with the default's label and ` — `; `--help` and `SPEC.md` say the label is added; a fixture and a test cover it; the precheck green.
